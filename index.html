04 / BEFORE YOU REACH OUT

GOOD
QUESTIONS.

Can you travel to my area?+

Ivan lists Las Vegas, Los Angeles, the Valley, Barstow, Arizona and Utah. Travel is arranged for each job. Send your city and vehicle details to confirm coverage and any travel costs.

Can every job be done at my location?+

It depends on the vehicle, repair, parts, equipment and available workspace. Motor swaps and transmission work need a suitable setting. Ivan will confirm what can be arranged.

Do I need to buy parts first?+

Speak with Ivan first. Share any parts you already have so you can confirm what’s needed, compatibility and the plan for the job.

How do I get a quote or appointment?+

Use the short request to prepare a text, or call (702) 786-8881. Pricing and appointments are confirmed directly. The form does not automatically book a visit or calculate a price.
